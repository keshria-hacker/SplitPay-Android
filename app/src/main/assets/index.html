What is SplitPay?

SplitPay lets you pay any merchant or person in multiple small UPI transfers — each ₹1,999 or less — instead of one large payment. This bypasses the NPCI MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) fee that banks charge merchants on UPI transactions above ₹2,000.

The app is entirely on-device: no servers, no sign-ups, no data uploaded anywhere. Your payment history lives only on this phone.

SplitPay opens your UPI app for each part in sequence. You enter your PIN for every transfer — each payment is a fully independent, secure UPI transaction. SplitPay never touches your bank account.
Get your receipt
After all parts are paid, you see a full receipt showing every part, the MDR you avoided, and a shareable summary. Everything is saved to your on-device Dashboard.
Why ₹1,999 per part?

NPCI's revised MDR framework charges acquiring banks a 0.4% MDR on UPI merchant payments of ₹2,000 and above — effective 15 October 2026. Transactions below that threshold, and merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh/month via UPI, remain fee-free. SplitPay keeps every part at ₹1,999 or less so the merchant never pays MDR on any single transfer.

Note: if the merchant you're paying already falls under that ₹1 lakh/month exemption, they weren't going to pay MDR on your payment either way — check with them if you're unsure.

Receive mode (for merchants)

Switch to Receive mode to generate a split-ready merchant QR. You can bundle up to 12 UPI accounts in one QR. When a SplitPay customer scans it, the parts are automatically spread across your accounts. Regular UPI apps that scan your QR pay only the primary (first) account.

Is this legal?
Yes. Splitting UPI payments is not prohibited by any NPCI, RBI, or bank regulation. Each transfer is a fully independent, legitimate UPI transaction. You are simply making multiple smaller payments instead of one large one.
Does the merchant get the same total?
Yes — the merchant receives every rupee. Each part hits their bank account separately. The total they receive equals the sum of all parts, which equals the amount you entered.
What if a part fails mid-way?
SplitPay tracks each part independently. If a part fails, you can retry it or skip it. The app shows exactly which parts succeeded and which didn't. A failed part never means the whole payment is reversed — only that specific part didn't go through.
Does SplitPay work with PhonePe / GPay / Paytm?
Yes — SplitPay generates standard upi:// links. Any installed UPI app that handles those links (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, and more) will open when SplitPay launches a part.
What is the maximum amount?
₹10,000 per split sequence (5 parts of ₹1,999 + one adjusted part). This is a product limit to keep sequences short. The underlying UPI limit depends on your bank.
What is Turbo Auto-Next?
In Auto-Run mode, Turbo launches the next part immediately after you mark the current one paid — no countdown delay. It's faster for people who pay back-to-back quickly.
What is the Random split mode?
Instead of equal parts (e.g. ₹1,667 × 3), Random generates distinct amounts (e.g. ₹982 + ₹1,104 + ₹1,414). Every amount is unique, which makes the payment pattern less predictable and may reduce bank-side fraud flags.
Can I use SplitPay without an internet connection?
Yes. The app and all its features work fully offline. The only network activity is the UPI deep link that opens your UPI app — and that's handled by the UPI app, not SplitPay.

Fully offline
SplitPay makes no network requests. It has no server, no API, and no backend. The only web request is loading this interface — the payment logic runs entirely on your phone.
Local storage only
Your UPI history, payment records, merchant accounts, and preferences are saved to your device's local storage (localStorage). They never leave your phone.
No account required
SplitPay never asks for your name, phone number, email, or any identity. There is nothing to sign up for.
No analytics or tracking
No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ad network. SplitPay cannot see how you use it.
Camera used only for QR scanning
Camera access is requested only when you tap "Open Camera." No frames are saved, recorded, or sent anywhere. The camera feed is decoded locally by jsQR (open source).
Deleting the app deletes your history
Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your payment history and saved settings. There is no cloud backup.
